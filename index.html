About Lovett Entertainment

Lovett Entertainment is all about moments — the first dance, the packed dance floor, the sing-along you’ll talk about for years. Based in Atlanta, GA, we bring professional sound, a clean setup, and a DJ who knows how to move every type of crowd.

Crowd Control

Reading the room is everything. From warm-up tracks to peak-hour anthems, the energy is guided so your event never feels flat.

Clean & Professional

On-time arrival, clean setup, and clear communication before, during, and after the event. You focus on the moment; we handle the music.

Custom Playlists

Every event is different. We work with your must-play songs, do-not-play list, and the exact feeling you want from start to finish.

Atlanta Roots

Born in the Atlanta sound — trap, R&B, old school, and more. If you want that real ATL flavor at your event, you’re in the right place.
