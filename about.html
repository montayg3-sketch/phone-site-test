About Lovett Entertainment

Atlanta-based DJ focused on making every event feel organized, smooth, and unforgettable — whether it’s a nightclub, a wedding reception, or a family celebration.

Professional from start to finish

Showing up early, communicating clearly, and being prepared are non-negotiable. Lovett Entertainment is built on professionalism — so you don’t have to worry about the DJ, you can focus on your guests.

We take time to understand the type of event you’re planning, who’s in the room, and what kind of energy you want throughout the night.

Reading the room

Every crowd is different. The playlist isn’t just a list of songs — it’s a live experience. We watch the room and adjust in real time to keep people engaged and on the dance floor.

From old school to new hits, clean versions to club vibes, Lovett Entertainment keeps the music moving in a way that fits your crowd.
